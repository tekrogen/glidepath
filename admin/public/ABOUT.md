# ABOUT

## Consumer personal-finance legal and privacy gate

Product boundary: Glidepath is intended to be an affordable, easy-to-use personal budgeting and household-finance application, comparable in product category to Monarch Money. Its beta provides read-only account aggregation, transaction tracking, budgeting, cash-flow and net-worth reporting, and descriptive educational insights.

Glidepath does not hold or move money, initiate payments or trades, extend credit, determine eligibility, manage assets, or provide professional financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Investment balances may be displayed, but beta functionality will not recommend trades, allocations, or specific investment actions. These boundaries must match the implemented product and its public claims before relying on them legally.

Documentation method: Glidepath will use a fact-first, attorney-assisted process rather than commissioning a full bespoke fintech package:

Prepare an internal data-flow, vendor, AI-use, retention, deletion, and household-access inventory.

Draft original plain-language documents based on Glidepath’s actual behavior and authoritative FTC/Plaid requirements.

Obtain limited-scope, preferably fixed-fee legal review covering regulatory classification, sensitive-data handling, household authorization, subscriptions, liability, disputes, and launch-state requirements.

Have Plaid-specific consent and disconnection behavior verified through the Plaid launch process.

The minimum beta package consists of:

* Concise Terms of Use.
* A layered Privacy Notice.
* A just-in-time Plaid disclosure and affirmative consent before Link.
* Household-sharing authorization explaining what each member can see and change.
* Accurate disconnection, retention, backup, and deletion explanations.
* Separate AI opt-in for any user financial data sent to an AI provider—or disabling that AI processing for beta.
* Subscription, renewal, cancellation, and refund terms before charging users.
* An internal vendor, subprocessor, and retention register.

Monarch’s public documents may be used as a coverage checklist but not copied. If Glidepath later introduces personalized investment recommendations, advisor/client functionality, credit decisions, or money movement, this must be reopened for broader legal review.
